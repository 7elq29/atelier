A · Edge Rail
Best pick for this app's aesthetic — invisible when not needed, richly informative on hover. Matches the "Soft Paper" principle of zero chrome by default. The 36px cost feels natural as the panel's "ghost."
B · Timeline Bar
Most immediately readable — the horizontal time metaphor is universally understood. Trade-off: 24px of vertical space always. Could be paired with A for "summary across top, detail on the right."
C · Topbar Pill
Most minimal and easiest to implement — enriches a button that's already there. Good starting point if the rail feels like too much. The "next task" info is the single most useful signal for most people.
Hybrid option worth considering

C + A combined: the topbar pill (C) is always visible and requires no hover — it shows the next task. The edge rail (A) adds depth for users who want the full day picture, and is invisible until hovered. These two reinforce each other: the pill answers "what's next now," the rail answers "how does the rest of the day look."
