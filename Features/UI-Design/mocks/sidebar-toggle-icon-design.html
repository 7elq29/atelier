Design notes
Static (sidebar open): unchanged — two-panel glyph, chevron left.

Dynamic (sidebar hidden):
  • 0 running — dim full circle, no number, no motion. A quiet resting state.
  • ≥1 running — spinning arc with a gap (~¼ turn) orbits the count. One rotation per 1.4 s.
  • Fully monochrome: only currentColor, no accent color. The motion is the signal.
  • The dim track circle behind the arc grounds the shape even when the count is 1.
  • Font shrinks from 7.5 px to 6 px at two digits (fits 10–99 without clipping).
